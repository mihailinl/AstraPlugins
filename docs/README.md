## Why the five are archived rather than updated

All 63 translated files landed in one commit and were never touched again, while
nine commits reshaped the CLI, the manifest, the proto and three SDKs. The audit
that started this rewrite found ~28 factually false claims in the English pages
alone; each of them existed seven times over, and nothing in CI could detect a
semantic drift in a language the maintainer cannot read — only a structural one.

So: English is canonical and half-generated, Russian is reviewed and covers the
written pages, and the other five sit in
[`docs/community/`](community/) behind a dated banner. They are not deleted,
because a translation that was correct in April is still worth more than nothing
to somebody who cannot read English — but they are not presented as current, and
a PR that refreshes one is welcome without being expected.

Nothing was translated until the English pages were correct. Translating first
is how twenty-eight errors became a hundred and ninety-six.

## For maintainers

| | |
|---|---|
| `docs/en/reference/` | Generated by `python3 tools/docgen/gen.py`. CI runs `--check` |
| `docs/en/parity.md`, `docs/en/hooks/` | Generated by `python3 tools/parity/gen.py` from `spec/hooks.yaml` |
| `docs/en/spec/` | Normative specifications, hand-written and reviewed |
| everything else under `docs/en/` and `docs/ru/` | Written, and every code sample in them is executed by `python3 docs/tools/doctest.py` |
